About

I'm a security researcher and CTF player. This site is where I write up machines after I root them — the full path, including the enumeration that went nowhere, because that's usually the part nobody else writes down.

Most of what's here is Hack The Box and TryHackMe writeups, with the occasional deeper research post when a technique is interesting enough to deserve its own writeup outside of a specific box. I also track security news that's actually worth your attention, and skip the rest.

If you spot a mistake in a writeup, or you want to talk about a chain, reach out — links are in the footer on every page.
